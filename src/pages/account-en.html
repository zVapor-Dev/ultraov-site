Delete your account

How to delete your Ultra OV NL account and the data that belongs to it. Lees in het Nederlands

An account is optional. If you use Ultra OV NL without one, there is nothing to do here: your data is stored only on your device.

In the app

  1. Open Ultra OV NL and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

Can't use the app anymore? Email support@ultraov.nl with the subject "Delete account", from your account's email address. We confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account details: email address, name (if you provided one) and sign-in details.
  • Your synced favourites and settings.

What is kept (temporarily)

  • Server logs with technical data, such as IP addresses. These are deleted automatically after at most 30 days.
  • Backups. Your data may remain in them for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten.
  • Your email conversations with support, for at most one year after your question was resolved.

Favourites and settings stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
