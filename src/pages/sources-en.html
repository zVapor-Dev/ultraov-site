Sources

Where the information in Ultra OV NL comes from, with a link to every original source. Lees in het Nederlands

Not a government app

Ultra OV NL is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Dutch government, any ministry, province or transport authority, NS or any transport operator. The information comes from public open data; the original sources are listed below. Data may differ from reality.

Official public transport data (government)

The Dutch public transport authorities publish their timetable and live data through the national access point NDOV Loket. These are the original sources of the government information in the app.

  • NDOV Loket: data.ndovloket.nl. Live vehicle positions (KV6), cancelled trips and detours (KV17), stop messages (KV15), NS train departures (InfoPlus DVS), the central stop register (CHB) and fares (NeTEx).
  • DOVA: dova.nu. The partnership of the regional public transport authorities (provinces and transport regions) that grant the bus, tram and metro concessions.
  • Government of the Netherlands: public transport: www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/openbaar-vervoer. Official government information about public transport in the Netherlands.
  • Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management: www.rijksoverheid.nl/ministeries/ministerie-van-infrastructuur-en-waterstaat. The ministry responsible for public transport policy and the main rail network.

Timetable and trains

  • OVapi: ovapi.nl. Departures per stop, the national timetable (GTFS-NL) and live changes (GTFS-realtime), via gtfs.ovapi.nl.
  • NS: www.ns.nl. Train departures, platforms and train fares are NS data, received through NDOV Loket.
  • Rijden de Treinen: www.rijdendetreinen.nl/open-data. Open data list of railway stations (names and codes).

Detours and notices

Detour articles come from the operators' own websites. Every article in the app links to the original.

  • Qbuzz / BUS Groningen Drenthe: busgd.nl. Detours in Groningen and Drenthe.
  • Qbuzz: www.qbuzz.nl. Operator website.
  • Arriva: www.arriva.nl. Operator website.
  • Connexxion: www.connexxion.nl. Operator website.
  • EBS: www.ebs-ov.nl. Operator website.
  • GVB: reisinfo.gvb.nl. Operator website.
  • RET: www.ret.nl. Operator website.
  • HTM: www.htm.nl/omleidingen-verstoringen. Operator website.
  • Keolis: www.keolis.nl. Operator website.

Official travel advice

Ultra OV NL can be wrong or out of date. Before an important trip, check the official travel information.

  • 9292: 9292.nl. Journey planner for all public transport in the Netherlands.
  • NS travel information: www.ns.nl/reizen. Official train travel information.
