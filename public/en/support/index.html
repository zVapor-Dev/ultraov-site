Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Lees in het Nederlands

Contact

Email support@ultraov.nl. If something is wrong, tell us which device and app version you use and what you were doing when it happened. If it is about a trip or vehicle, include the stop, line and time.

Ultra OV NL is a small, independent project. We reply as soon as we can, usually within a few working days.

Frequently asked questions

A departure time or vehicle is wrong. How can that be?

Ultra OV NL uses open Dutch public transport data. Not every trip has a live signal, and data sometimes arrives late or incomplete. We never fill in unknown data ourselves. If you spot a recurring error, email us.

Can I buy a ticket or file a complaint with the operator through the app?

No. Ultra OV NL is not affiliated with any transport operator. For tickets, refunds and complaints about a journey, contact the operator directly.

Where did my favourites go?

Without an account, favourites are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. With an account, they are synced.

How do I turn off location access?

In your device settings, under the Ultra OV NL app. Searching by name keeps working.

Deleting your account or data

If you have an account, you can delete it in the app or at ultraov.nl/en/delete-account. Without an account, your data lives on your device: uninstall the app or clear its data. Server logs are deleted automatically after at most 30 days. Read more in the privacy policy.
