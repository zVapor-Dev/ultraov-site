Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Lees in het Nederlands

In short: Ultra OV NL has no ads and no trackers. An account is optional. Without one, your favourites and settings stay on your own device. We only use your location when you ask for nearby stops yourself.

This privacy policy covers the Ultra OV NL apps for Android and Apple devices and the website ultraov.nl (together: "Ultra OV NL"). It explains what data we process, why, and what rights you have. The Dutch version is the original; if the two differ, the Dutch version applies.

Who we are

Ultra OV NL is the controller for the processing of personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at support@ultraov.nl.

What data we process

Data that stays on your device

Without an account, your favourite stops and vehicles, language, theme, layout and visible tabs are stored only on your own device. We do not receive this data. If you uninstall the app, it is removed as well.

Optional account

You can use Ultra OV NL without an account. If you do create one, we process:

  • your email address and, if you provide one, a name;
  • your sign-in details: a hashed password, or a user ID from the service you sign in with, such as Google or Apple;
  • the favourites and settings you choose to sync between your devices;
  • when your account was created and when you last signed in.

If you sign in with another service, such as Google or Apple, we only receive what you allow that service to share: usually your email address, a user ID and sometimes your name. We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time: see Delete your account.

Requests to our server

To show travel information, the app sends requests to our server. Depending on what you do, a request contains:

  • a search term, or the stop, station, line, trip or vehicle you look up;
  • the origin and destination (and an optional via point), departure time and travel preferences when you plan a journey;
  • your location (coordinates), only when you ask for nearby stops or choose your current location as origin or destination.

We use this data only to answer your request. As with almost any internet service, the server also receives technical data: your IP address, the time, the requested address and the type of app or browser.

Location

The app only asks for location access when you use a feature that needs it, such as nearby stops. You can refuse, or turn it off later in your device settings; searching by name keeps working. We do not track your location in the background and do not build a location history.

Server logs

Our server keeps a log of requests (with the technical data above, including IP address and the requested address). We use it only to fix problems and prevent abuse. Logs are deleted automatically after at most 30 days. We do not link them to a person and do not use them for profiling or advertising.

Contacting support

If you email us, we use your email address and message to help you. We keep this correspondence no longer than needed, and at most one year after your question has been resolved.

The website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. When you visit it, the same technical data is processed as described under server logs.

What we don't do

  • We show no ads and do not use an advertising ID.
  • We use no third-party analytics or tracking services in the app or on the website.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.
  • You don't need an account. Without one, we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Where your data is stored

Ultra OV NL runs on a privately operated server. The website and server are reached through Cloudflare, which routes traffic and protects against attacks. In doing so, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address on our behalf. Cloudflare may process data outside the European Economic Area; appropriate safeguards apply, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Third-party data

The travel information in the app comes from open Dutch public transport data sources, including NDOV Loket, OVapi, GTFS and NS. This is data about timetables, trips and vehicles, not personal data about you. The app does not send any data about you to these sources.

You download the app from Google Play or the App Store. Google and Apple process data under their own privacy policies.

Children

Ultra OV NL is meant for a general audience and is not specifically aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 beyond what is described above.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, restrict processing, object, and receive your data in a portable format. If you have an account, email us from that account's address. Data without an account, such as server logs, can only be linked to you with your help, for example your IP address and the time.

Send your request to support@ultraov.nl. We reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: delete it in the app under Settings, or follow the steps on Delete your account. Your account data and synced favourites are then deleted; they disappear from backups within 30 days.
  • On your device: clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Your favourites and settings are then gone.
  • On our server: server logs are deleted automatically after at most 30 days. If you want them deleted sooner, email support@ultraov.nl.

More details are on the Support and contact page.

Security

All connections between the app, the website and our server are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are only stored hashed. Access to the server, account data and logs is limited to the administrator of Ultra OV NL.

Changes

If the app changes, for example when we add a feature that uses different data, we update this policy before that change goes live. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
